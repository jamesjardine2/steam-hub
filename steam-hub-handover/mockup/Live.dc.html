Year 1/2 · Weave It Stronger Together: Paper Makers Exit

Learning Intention

To weave paper strips together and test whether weaving makes paper stronger.

Success Criteria: I can

weave strips over and under on a paper loom test a woven mat against loose strips explain why the woven mat is stronger
[Photo: woven basket holding something heavy]

Think about it

One paper strip rips easily. How can a basket made of thin strips hold all this?

How to weave

1 Over, under, over, under
2 Push it to the top
3 Check: does it look like a checkerboard?
