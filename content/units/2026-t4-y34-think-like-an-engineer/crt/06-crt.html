Lesson 6: LEGO Design Studio

Year 3/4 STEAM | Unit: Think Like an Engineer: Design Challenge Series | CRT overview | 45 minutes

No preparation and no screens. Students choose one of three LEGO challenges, brainstorm aloud, make a quick sketch on scrap paper, then spend most of the lesson building and improving, on their own or with a partner. LEGO tubs, base plates and marbles are on the STEAM room shelves. Use a hardcover book to test stands, not an iPad.

Learning Intention and Success Criteria

Learning Intention:

To design and build a LEGO product that meets the design criteria for the person who will use it.

Success Criteria:

I can:

  • choose a challenge and say who my design is for.
  • build a design that meets the design criteria.
  • test my design against the criteria and improve it.

Write the three challenges and their criteria on the board. User means the person the design is for. Design criteria are what the design must do for the user.

The lesson in brief

TimeWhat to do
0 to 7 minAsk: "Last week, what did you change in your helicopter?" Lean a book against a pencil case and let it slide: "Is this a good stand? Who is it for? What does it need to do?" Read the Learning Intention and Success Criteria.
7 to 13 minRead each challenge and its criteria. Students show 1, 2 or 3 fingers to choose, and choose to work alone or with a partner.
13 to 16 minBrainstorm: talk only, hands off the bricks. Share ideas with a partner or the table.
16 to 20 minSketch: hand out scrap paper. A quick sketch of the idea to build first (not kept).
20 to 38 minBuild and improve. At about 29 minutes: "Hands off. Thumbs for each criterion. Fix your weakest part first, and ask a classmate to try it."
38 to 45 min2 or 3 students share: "My ___ is for ___. It meets ___. I improved ___ because ___." Then builds taken apart, bricks back in the tubs, marbles and base plates counted back.
Key idea to finish on: Engineers design for a user. A design is good when it meets the user's criteria, and the best way to find out is to let someone use it.

Notes for the teacher

  • Marbles catch on LEGO studs: build the path from smooth flat tiles, or use a ping pong ball. Count marbles out and back in.
  • Students who are stuck: start with the desk organiser, one thing at a time. Scrap paper and pencils for the sketch are in the room.
  • Please leave a short note about how far the class got and any students who were absent.
