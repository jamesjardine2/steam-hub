Stay safe

Water stays in the tub. Wipe spills straight away. Fold over sharp foil edges.

Learning Intention

To predict how many pebbles a foil boat will hold, then test it fairly and improve the design.

Success Criteria: I can

write a prediction before I test. build a boat that follows the challenge constraints. compare my result with my prediction and explain one change I would make.

The challenge

Build a boat that holds the most pebbles.

One square of foil. No tape. 1 minute to plan, 4 minutes to build. Three rounds.

Round 1: test

Gently on the water. One pebble at a time, in the middle. Count out loud. Write your result.

Round 2: test

Gently on the water. One pebble at a time, in the middle. Count out loud. Write your result.
