Stay safe

LEGO stays on the table. Pick up dropped bricks straight away. Test stands with the book first.

Learning Intention

To design and build a LEGO product that meets the design criteria for the person who will use it.

Success Criteria: I can

choose a challenge and say who my design is for. build a design that meets the design criteria. test my design against the criteria and improve it.

Two words

user

the person the design is for

design criteria

what the design must do for the user

A. iPad stand

For someone watching a video at their desk

Holds the iPad for a count of 10.The user can see the screen.Stays up when the table is bumped.

B. Desk organiser

For a student whose pencils are always lost

Holds 4 pencils, a rubber and a ruler.Each thing has its own place.Take each thing out with one hand.

C. Marble maze

For a friend to solve

A start and a finish.The marble cannot escape.At least three turns.
