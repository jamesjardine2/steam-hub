Stronger Together: Paper Makers

Remember

Last week, how did we make paper stronger?

We wove strips over and under to make a strong mat.

Think about it

Could flour stick paper together? Could water?

Thumbs up?Thumbs down?

Watch

Flour can't do it. Water can't do it.

What if we mix them together? Count the stirs with me!

Before we start

Flour and paste are for sticking, not for eating.

Do not taste the flour or the paste. Keep your hands away from your mouth and eyes. Wash your hands when we finish.

Learning Intention

To find out that mixing flour and water makes paste, a new material with different properties.

Success Criteria: I can

describe what flour and water are like on their own. mix them to make paste. test which one sticks paper best.
