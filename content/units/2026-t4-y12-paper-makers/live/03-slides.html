Oh no!

The strips slid apart and the pebbles fell.

How could we make strips stay together without glue or tape?

Look closely

Look at the sleeve of your jumper or shirt.

Tiny threads go over and under each other to make fabric. That is weaving!

Weaving for thousands of years

Wurundjeri weavers make baskets from grass fibres.

This binak (basket) was made by Wurundjeri weaver Mikaela Rose Cottingham. What could you carry in it?

Learning Intention

To weave strips of paper together to make a material that is stronger than the strips on their own.

Success Criteria: I can

weave paper strips over and under. test how many pebbles my woven mat can carry. explain how my woven mat is different from loose strips.
