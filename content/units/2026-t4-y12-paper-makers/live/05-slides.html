Stronger Together: Paper Makers

Remember

We made paste, then stuck three paper strips into layers.

Think about it

Which strip will hold more blocks?

Thumbs up: singleThumbs down: layered

Watch

Add blocks one at a time. Count with me!

Stop when the strip sags and touches the table.

Before we start

Test over the table and keep the blocks safe.

Keep the cup of blocks away from the edge. Add blocks gently, one at a time. If blocks fall, pick them up straight away.

Learning Intention

To design a paper carrier for a toy by choosing techniques that make paper stronger.

Success Criteria: I can

test a layered strip and a single strip to see which holds more. draw a carrier for my toy with a base, sides and a handle. label the techniques I will use.
