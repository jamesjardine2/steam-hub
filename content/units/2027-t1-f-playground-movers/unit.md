Pushes and pulls are forces that make things move. Students move their bodies, then classroom objects, then plan, build, test and share a toy for a playground, moving from concrete to abstract. The digital work stays small (photo, video and one Book Creator page) so the science stays the focus.

Term 1 also carries the PCMS and SWPBS focus. Lessons 1 to 4 are matched to PCMS Weeks 2 to 5, and each one teaches its behaviour focus in the Foundation way: I show you and we practise together. [Check the lesson dates against the PCMS weeks when the 2027 timetable is set.]
