No preparation is needed. Everything is in the **labelled tub on the STEAM desk**: the handouts (one per student, plus one for you to hold up), the Design challenge cards already cut into sets, scissors and glue sticks. STEAM journals are in the class tub. Bring the class straight to the mat and start with the hook.

**Focus:** Homes come in lots of shapes and sizes, and every home keeps people safe, dry and comfy.

**Space:** Mat area for the hook and discussion, then tables for cutting and pasting.

## Lesson sequence

| Time | Step | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | Hook (CIC) | **Mystery home riddle.** Read the riddle slowly, one clue at a time, and let students guess after each clue: "People sleep in me every night. I have a kitchen and a bed. I have wheels, and a car can pull me to the beach." When someone says caravan, point to the caravan on the handout. Ask: "Is this a home? How do you know?" |
| 5 to 15 min | Talk about the pictures | Point to each picture on the handout. Students show thumbs up (someone lives here) or thumbs down (nobody lives here). Ask one quick question per picture, for example "Where would they sleep?" or "How would this keep the rain out?" Then ask a tricky one with no picture: "What about a hotel? People sleep there. Is it a home?" Let partners talk, then share. Finish with the big idea, said together: **"Every home keeps people safe, dry and comfy."** |
| 15 to 20 min | Model the journal | Show students how to set up their STEAM journal: open to a new double page. The **tick label** (Someone lives here) is glued at the top of the left page. The **cross label** (Nobody lives here) is glued at the top of the right page. Model cutting out one picture, deciding which side it goes, and gluing it. While you model, choose one helper per table to put out scissors, glue sticks, journals and a set of Design challenge cards (face down). |
| 20 to 38 min | Cut, sort, stick | At tables, students write their name in their journal, then cut out the 2 labels and all 9 pictures. They **sort the pictures on the table first**, then glue them into their journal under the correct label. Walk around and ask each student: "Why did you put this one here? What keeps the people dry?" **Early finishers:** choose a Design challenge card. Read it to them, then they draw a home for that person on the next journal page and tell you about the feature that helps. Write a label for them if needed. |
| 38 to 45 min | Share and reflect | Choose 3 or 4 students to show their journal and explain one picture. Read the Success Criteria aloud and students show a thumb (up, sideways or down) for each one. Pack away scissors and glue, and collect journals. |

## The handout

One A4 sheet per student with 2 labels and 9 pictures to cut out. There are no words on the pictures, so students have to look and think to sort them.

| Group | Pictures |
|---|---|
| Someone lives here (5) | Suburban house, apartment building, caravan, camping tent, house on stilts |
| Nobody lives here (4) | School, supermarket, bus, playground |

## Questions to ask

- Where would the people sleep?
- What keeps the rain and wind out?
- What is this home made of?
- Why would someone live up high on stilts? (to stay dry if the river floods)
- Why might someone choose a home with wheels?

## What to look for

| Success criterion | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|
| Sorts homes and not-homes | All 9 pictures glued under the correct label in the STEAM journal. |
| Says what makes a home | Uses words like sleep, live, safe, dry, warm, family when explaining a choice. |
| Tells how a home keeps people safe or dry | Points to a roof, walls, door or stilts and says what it does. |

## Design challenge cards

These are for students who finish the sort early and are ready to think like designers. Read the card aloud. A strong response is a home with a feature that matches the person's need.

| Card | A home for... | Look for a feature like |
|---|---|---|
| Flood | Someone who lives next to a river that floods | Stilts, stairs up to the door, a house that floats |
| On the move | Someone who moves around a lot | Wheels, a tent that folds up, something light to carry |
| Very hot | Someone who lives somewhere very hot | Shade, a verandah, big windows for breeze, trees |

## Support and extension

- **Support:** Pre-cut the pictures for students who find cutting hard. Give the sentence starter: "This is a home because people ___ here."
- **Extension:** Design challenge cards (see above). Students who finish a card can choose a second one.

## Notes for the CRT

- **Sort before you stick.** Foundation students glue fast, so remind them to lay every picture on the table under the right label before any glue comes out.
- **A dot, not a lot** of glue on the back of each picture.
- If time is short, skip the hotel question.
- Please leave the **STEAM journals** in the class tub on the STEAM desk. A short note about how the class went is always appreciated.
