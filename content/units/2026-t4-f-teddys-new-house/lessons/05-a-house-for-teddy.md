**Setup:** Put the Lesson 4 result chart where students can see it. Hand out journals and pencils at the tables. Draw a blank house outline area on the board ready for the model plan. [Check the STEAM cupboard for a LEGO door or hinge piece to show.]

**Focus:** Builders plan before they build. A plan is a drawing with labels that shows what we will make.

**Links:** In Lesson 3 students made strong walls and in Lesson 4 they tested materials to find the best roof. Now they put both ideas into a plan for Teddy's house, which they build in Lesson 6.

## Lesson sequence

| Time | Step | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | Hook (CIC) | Ask the recall question: "Which material kept Teddy dry?" Then: "Teddy is ready to build his house. What should we do first?" Take a few ideas. Ask: "What parts does his house need?" Students tell a partner. Read the Learning Intention and the Success Criteria. |
| 5 to 10 min | Word wall | Use the word wall. A **plan** is a drawing of what you will build. A **label** is a word that names a part. A **hinge** lets a door swing open. Students think first, then the box is uncovered. |
| 10 to 17 min | I do | Show the how-to slides, then draw a plan on the board and think aloud. "First the walls. Now the roof. I will colour it the best material from our test. Now a door. This door must open, so I draw which way it swings. A window, so Teddy has light. Now I label each part with a word." |
| 17 to 32 min | You do | Students draw and label their own plan in their journal. Walk through the steps with the class, a part at a time: walls, roof, door, window, labels. Write labels for students who need it. [Add how many labels each student should aim for.] |
| 32 to 40 min | Share | Students tell a partner what each part is for, using the starter: "This is the ___. It is for ___." Take a few plans to the mat. Say together: **"A plan shows what to build."** |
| 40 to 45 min | Pack up | Pencils and journals back in the tub. Teaser: "Next week we build Teddy's house from our plans." |

## What to draw on the board

Draw a model plan: a rectangle for the walls, a triangle roof coloured the best material, a door with a curved arrow showing which way it swings, a window and four labels (roof, door, window, wall) with lines pointing to each part.

## Questions to ask

- What part are you drawing now? What is it for?
- Which material did you choose for the roof? Why?
- Which way does your door open?
- Where is the hinge on your door?
- How does a label help the builder?

## What to look for

| Success criterion | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|
| Draws the parts | The plan has walls, a roof, a door that opens and a window. |
| Chooses the roof material | Says or shows the material that kept the water out in Lesson 4. |
| Labels the parts | Words or letters beside the parts, with a line pointing to each one. |

## Support and extension

- **Support:** Give the sentence starter "This is the ___." Draw the first part together, and write the label words on a card to copy.
- **Extension:** Ask students to add a second room or a chimney, or to show where the hinges are on the door.

## The key idea

A plan is a labelled drawing that shows what we will build. We plan first so that the house has everything Teddy needs.

## Notes for the CRT

- Everything needed is journals and pencils. No water or LEGO is used in this lesson.
- Students who finish early can add details such as a path or a garden.
- Please leave the **STEAM journals** in the class tub on the STEAM desk. A short note about how the class went is always appreciated.
