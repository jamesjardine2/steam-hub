**Setup:** Set out the trays, cups of water and droppers from Lesson 4, with paper towel squares. Put the plans, card doors and plastic roofs from the class tray on the tables, with spares and tape strips. Have the photos from Lesson 7 ready in case a pair cannot read its plan.

**Focus:** Designers test what they make and improve it, and they give each other kind, useful feedback. A good plan lets you build the same house again.

**Links:** The rain test is the same test as Lesson 4, now on the whole house. The rebuild shows why we plan: the plan from Lesson 5, with the changes drawn on in Lesson 7, tells each pair how to build their house again.

## Lesson sequence

| Time | Step | What to do and say |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 min | Hook (CIC) | Show a finished house. Put a paper towel square inside: "This is Teddy's bed." Hold up the dropper: "Rain is coming. Will Teddy's bed stay dry? Think... thumbs up or down." Read the Learning Intention and Success Criteria. |
| 3 to 7 min | Word wall | Use the word wall. A **test** is trying something to find out if it works. **Improve** means change something to make it better. **Feedback** is telling someone what you think of their work, kindly. Students think first, then each box is uncovered. For each non-example, ask "Why is this not ___?" and use the reasons below. |
| 7 to 19 min | Rebuild | Pairs rebuild their house from their plan: base, walls with overlap and a doorway, door, roof. "Your plan tells you how to build it again." |
| 19 to 29 min | Rain test | Model once: bed inside, roof on, 10 drops on the roof while the class counts, lift the roof, look at the bed. Then pairs test on a tray. Before dripping, each pair predicts with thumbs. After, thumbs up for dry, thumbs down for wet. |
| 29 to 33 min | Improve | Pairs with a wet bed look for where the water got in (the roof edge, a gap at the door, a hole between bricks), fix it with tape or a brick, and test again with a fresh bed. Pairs with a dry bed tell a partner which part kept Teddy dry. |
| 33 to 40 min | House tour | Half the pairs stay with their house and half visit, then swap. Visitors look, open the door and say: **"I like your ___ because ___."** Then they hold up a smiley face card. |
| 40 to 45 min | Pack up | Tip out water, wipe trays, pull the houses apart and put the bricks back. Say together: **"We plan, build, test and improve."** |

## Word wall: why each non-example is not the word

Say these after students have had a go at explaining.

- **Test.** A guess: it comes before; you have not tried it yet. Just looking at the roof: looking is not trying it out. Building the house: that is making it, not finding out if it works.
- **Improve.** Knocking the house down: it changes it, but it does not make it better. Leaving it the same: nothing changes. Taping the door shut: it changes it, but now the door cannot open, so it is not better.
- **Feedback.** "I like your shoes": it is not about their work. "It's bad": it is not kind and it does not help. Talking about your own house: it is not about their work.

## Questions to ask

- Do you think Teddy's bed will stay dry? Why?
- Where did the water get in? How can you fix it?
- What did you change? Is it better now?
- Which part of your friend's house do you like? Why?
- How did your plan help you build it again?

## What to look for

| Success criterion | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|
| Tests the house | Predicts, drips 10 drops, lifts the roof and says if the bed is dry or wet. |
| Says one improvement | Points to where water got in, or names one change that would make the house better. |
| Gives kind feedback | Uses the stem about a part of the friend's house, with a reason. |

## Support and extension

- **Support:** Rebuild alongside the pair using the photo from Lesson 7, and give the stem with a choice: "I like your door / roof / walls because ___."
- **Extension:** Try a heavier rain test (20 drops, or drops near the door), then improve the house and draw the change onto the plan.

## The key idea

We plan, build, test and improve. A good plan lets us build our house again, and a test tells us if it works.

## Notes for the CRT

- Water stays on the trays. Wipe spills straight away.
- If the houses were kept from Lesson 7, skip the rebuild and give the extra time to the test and the house tour.
- Please leave the plans in the STEAM journals in the class tub.
