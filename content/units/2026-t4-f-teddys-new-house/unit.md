Homes from the outside (Lesson 1) and the inside (Lesson 2), then Teddy arrives needing a new house. Students build him strong walls, test roof materials to keep him dry, plan his house, make a door that opens on a hinge, build it from LEGO, card and plastic, then test it in the rain and take the class on a house tour.

Every lesson is simple, fits in 45 minutes and can be taught by a CRT with minimal resources. Lessons 1 and 2 were written for a CRT.
