Big idea

Overlap the bricks to make a strong wall.

Learning Intention

To learn that the way we join bricks together can make a house strong.

Success Criteria: I can

overlap my bricks to build a strong wall. build a house with walls and a door with my partner. test my house and fix any parts that break.
