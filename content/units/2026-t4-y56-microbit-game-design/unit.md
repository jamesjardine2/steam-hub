Year 5 meet the micro:bit and Year 6 refresh it, then everyone learns the Jacdac plug-and-play system (KittenBot Jacdac Starter Kit A with micro:bit V2, coded in the micro:bit iPad app). Students code Button Smash on the micro:bit alone, rebuild it with Jacdac buttons, lights and sound, then code a reaction game and design a control panel that a brand new player can use without being told how. The unit ends with an arcade day.

All coding is on iPads in the micro:bit app, simulator first, with downloads over Bluetooth. The Pet Hamster lesson in Files and links is a ready-made CRT backup.
