Lesson 5

Reaction Game

Remember

How did lights and sound help the player?

The game

Press start. Wait for green. Hit it!

Design brief

Design a control panel a new player can use without being told how.

Learning Intention

To find the problems players have with our game and plan a control panel that solves them.

Success Criteria: I can

connect and run my game code. identify problems a new player has when using the game. sketch a control panel layout and explain why I put each part where I did.

Think about it

Would a Prep student know how to play this?

List every moment you were confused.

Our criteria

About the player, not the looks.

Could we check it by watching someone play?

Plan your panel

Sketch two layouts. Label why each part goes there. Circle one. Link it to a criterion.
