Lesson 7

Arcade Day

Learning Intention

To evaluate our games against our design criteria and share what we have learned.

Success Criteria: I can

play other pairs' games and give feedback using our criteria. use feedback to make one final improvement. explain how my game uses inputs, outputs and variables.

Arcade rules

Play without being told how. Leave a keep and a change. Stay-behind partner: watch, don't explain.

Round 1

Play two games.

Then swap roles.

Round 2

Play two games.

Then swap roles.

Round 3

Play two games.

Then swap roles.

Feedback

Keep: ___ because ___. Change: ___ because ___.

Evaluate

Which criterion does your game meet best? Evidence? Which was hardest? What would you change? Label one input, one output and one variable.
