Lesson 2

Build and Pitch

Remember

Who is your user? What do they need?

Learning Intention

To finish our design and use our design criteria to evaluate it and other designs.

Success Criteria: I can

label how three features of my design help my user. give feedback on another design that is linked to a criterion. explain one way my design meets the criteria and one way I could improve it.

Design criteria

It helps our user. It fits the place. It is safe. It lasts and wastes little.

Finish it

Three features on your card.

Feature ___ helps our user because ___.

Two stars and a wish

Star: it ___ because ___. Star: it ___ because ___. Wish: it could ___ so that ___.

Name a criterion every time.

Gallery walk

Move one table clockwise. Read the card, then look. Look, don't touch.

Improve it

Read your notes. Write one improvement.

Pitch it

Our design is ___. It is for ___. It helps them by ___.
