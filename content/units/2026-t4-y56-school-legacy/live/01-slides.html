Lesson 1

Legacy Project

Think about it

What should FCPS have after you've gone?

Legacy

Something you leave behind that helps the people who come after you.

Learning Intention

To find out what a user at our school needs and start a design that meets that need.

Success Criteria: I can

describe who my design is for and what they need. explain why each of our class design criteria matters. sketch a design with three labelled features and start building it.

The brief

Design something for FCPS that helps a real user.

Choose a user. Choose a real place at school. Design for what they need.

Who is it for?

Foundation children The whole school Students who need a quiet space The garden and its animals

Turn and talk

Our user needs ___ because ___.

Design criteria

It helps our user. It fits the place. It is safe. It lasts and wastes little.

Example

A buddy bench for Foundation

Bright colour Picture sign Shade from a tree
