Some preparation needed: hide a sound source in a box or bag where students cannot see it. Choose one sound to be the room's signal and use only that sound for it, all lesson and all term. Walk the room beforehand and decide where the out of bounds areas are. [Out of bounds areas and the toilet and drink rule to add from James.]

## How the activity works

- **Mystery first:** play the hidden sound without showing the source. Ask what made it. Take three or four guesses, then show the source.
- **The sound is a signal:** say "That sound is our secret signal. When you hear it, you freeze and listen."
- **Freeze and listen:** students walk slowly around the room. At the signal they freeze, look at James and listen for the job.
- **Three parts of a lesson:** each lesson has a mini lesson (listen and watch), a task (do the work) and a pack up (tidy and sit). The signal tells us when each part changes.
- **Boundaries:** the tour finds the out of bounds areas. A student helper shows how to stop at the line. The toilet and drink rule is practised once.

**Key words:** expectation, signal, boundary

## Lesson sequence

| Time | Step | What to do and say |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 7 min | Hook | Students sit on the floor. Ask: "You already know how to learn in your classroom. What might be different here?" Take two answers. Then play the mystery sound from the hidden box. Ask: "What made that sound?" Take guesses, then show the source. Say: "This sound is our room's signal." |
| 7 to 15 min | I do | Read the Learning Intention and Success Criteria aloud. Show the word wall for **expectation**, **signal** and **boundary**. Model freezing and listening when the signal plays, then say what you did: "I stopped, I looked, I listened." Show the three picture cards and say what happens in a mini lesson, a task and a pack up. |
| 15 to 23 min | We do: freeze and listen | Students walk slowly around the room. Play the signal. They freeze and look at you. Repeat three times. Ask: "What did you do when you heard it? How did you stop?" Students say the steps aloud. |
| 23 to 33 min | We do: tour | Lead the class around the room with a sound cue to move on and a sound cue to stop. At each out of bounds area, a student helper stands at the line. Ask: "Why do we stay out of here?" Then practise the toilet and drink rule once. [Rule to add.] |
| 33 to 40 min | You do | Pairs choose a card (mini lesson, task or pack up) and act it out with no words. The class guesses which one. |
| 40 to 45 min | Review | Pair share: "One thing we did today that helped us learn." Finish with the key idea. Students stop at the signal and walk out. |

## Draw this on the board

Three boxes in a row: **Mini lesson**, **Task**, **Pack up**, each with a small picture.

Sentence stem: **In our room I can learn safely and kindly when I ______.**

## Questions to ask while roaming

- What did you do when you heard the signal?
- What do you do in a mini lesson? In a task? In a pack up?
- Which areas are out of bounds? Why?
- What do you do if you need the toilet or a drink?
- How did that help everyone learn?

## Support and extension

- **Support:** Pair students with a partner who can show the freeze. Use the picture cards so students can point instead of say.
- **Extension:** Students make up a new signal job and say what it would tell the class to do, for example "two sounds means come to the floor".

**Key idea to finish on:** A sound can be a signal. When we hear it, we freeze and listen, so everyone can learn safely and kindly.

## Notes for the teacher

- **Safety:** keep the tour slow and walking. Check the out of bounds areas for hazards before the lesson.
- **Keep the signal the same:** use only one sound for the signal so students link it with freezing. Later lessons add more sounds for other jobs.
- **Privacy:** keep names out of anything recorded. Talk about the class, not about any student.
